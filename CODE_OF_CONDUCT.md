# Code of Conduct

This is a small technical repository. The standard is simple: argue about the work, not
about the person doing it.

---

## Expected behaviour

1. Critique ideas, code, and wording directly. Blunt technical disagreement is welcome and
   is the point of a review.
2. Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until the evidence says
   otherwise.
3. When you are wrong, say so and move on. No one is keeping score.
4. Respect a maintainer's decision to decline a change. Scope control is a feature of this
   repository, not an insult to your proposal.

## Unacceptable behaviour

1. Personal attacks, demeaning comments, or remarks targeting someone's identity,
   background, or level of experience.
2. Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or private messages
   arising from activity here.
3. Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
4. Sustained disruption — reopening settled decisions, brigading a thread, or repeatedly
   ignoring maintainer direction.

## Scope

This applies to every space this project occupies: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and any private correspondence that starts from participation here.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private report through the repository's **Security → Report
a vulnerability** form, which is private to maintainers, or by contacting the maintainer
directly at **[maintainer contact]**.

Reports are reviewed privately. Responses scale with severity:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was out of line.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with a defined period of no interaction with the people
   involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — removal from all project spaces for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for sustained or severe violations.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith are subject to the same consequences.
